Signing in
{{- if .Values.admin.create }}
  The first administrator is "{{ .Values.admin.username }}". Its starting password:
    kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "plinth.adminSecretName" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.{{ include "plinth.adminSecretKey" . }}}' | base64 -d; echo
